# Initial User Jobs

These user jobs are derived from the seed corpus. They describe recovery and understanding tasks rather than product features.

## UJ-001 — Recover current truth

When several artifacts describe the same engineering object, determine what is authoritative now and why.

Typical questions:

- What is the current state?
- Which representation owns that state?
- Which other representations are stale or historical?

## UJ-002 — Recover historical truth

Reconstruct what was believed, authorized, configured, or valid at a particular point in project history without confusing it with current truth.

## UJ-003 — Explain why an artifact or implementation exists

Traverse from implementation or work output back through the work packet, specification, architectural decisions, requirements, evidence, and original rationale that caused it to exist.

## UJ-004 — Explain why something changed

Identify the prior state, triggering evidence or event, decision/correction, affected artifacts, and resulting state.

## UJ-005 — Reconstruct provenance

Determine where information came from, what source state it refers to, which context produced it, and whether the durable record accurately reflects what happened.

## UJ-006 — Compare representations of the same logical object

See canonical, projected, generated, historical, and external representations together and understand their roles and synchronization relationships.

## UJ-007 — Determine evidence validity

For an assertion or decision, identify supporting evidence, the state actually evaluated, the proposition the validator checked, and whether later relevant change invalidated that support.

## UJ-008 — Reconstruct an execution or work episode

Recover the work packet, authorization state, execution identity, baseline, branch/worktree, implementation diff, defects encountered, evidence, review, and closure outcome as one intelligible episode.

## UJ-009 — Diagnose contradictory information

Determine whether disagreement is caused by error, temporal change, stale projection, context difference, authority scope, semantic mismatch, or genuinely competing claims.

## UJ-010 — Determine what a derived artifact came from

Trace a projection, generated document, machine record, report, or summary to its source inputs and derivation event.

## UJ-011 — Return after loss of mental context

After days or months away from the project, recover enough of the relevant decision path, current state, open questions, and work history to continue without rereading the entire repository or conversation history.

## UJ-012 — Learn the engineering process as a narrative

Follow a coherent path from problem through questions, alternatives, experiments, decisions, implementation, failures, corrections, and lessons without requiring the reader to know the repository's internal filing system first.

## UJ-013 — Assess impact before changing something

Starting from a requirement, decision, component, source artifact, or work item, discover which other knowledge and artifacts depend on it and what might become stale or inconsistent if it changes.

## UJ-014 — Preserve a correction without erasing the mistake

Record that earlier project knowledge was incomplete or wrong, preserve what was believed and why, link the correction and evidence, and make the corrected state clearly authoritative going forward.
